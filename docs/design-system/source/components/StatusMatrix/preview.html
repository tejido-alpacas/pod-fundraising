C · Confirmation strength

Participant confirmation

Confirmed by someone involved in the work or paid by the project, such as the organizer.

Stakeholder confirmation

Confirmed by the beneficiary or a local stakeholder. Not paid by the project, but not neutral.

Independent confirmation

Confirmed by a third party with no stake in the project.
