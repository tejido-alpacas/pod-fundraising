Rebuild the library wing

For the 214 pupils of Mangrove Bay Primary School. Organized by the Mangrove Bay Parent-Teacher Association.

The hurricane took the roof and two walls off the library wing at Mangrove Bay Primary School. This campaign pays to rebuild the wing on a new foundation and to restock its shelves.
