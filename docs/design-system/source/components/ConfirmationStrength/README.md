# ConfirmationStrength

Who confirmed, and how independent they are: participant, stakeholder or independent.

- **Provide:** the category from the confirmer's recorded relationship, plus who they are and why that category applies ("Employed by the parish council. Not paid by the project.").
- The one-to-three thread mark is a category marker, not a score. Never add numbers, stars or percentages.
- Default unknown relationships to stakeholder, never independent. An organizer sign-off (`confirmed_creator`) is always participant.
- Use the compact form in invitations and review screens, and the full form with reasons on milestone and proof pages.
